CUSTOM WELCOME / LEAVE IMAGE SYSTEM

Welcome:
  /setwelcome channel:#welcome image:https://example.com/welcome.png

Leave:
  /setleave channel:#goodbye image:https://example.com/goodbye.png

The image URL is saved in data.json under the guild configuration and is used by:
- Automatic welcome messages
- Automatic goodbye messages
- /welcometest
- /leavetest

The image option is optional. Only valid http/https URLs are applied.
